## Supervision vs control (critical distinction)

From RA 7160 policy of local autonomy:

- **General supervision** = ensure barangay acts are within scope of prescribed powers.
- **Not** = mayor may veto barangay legislation outright (except EO review per Sec. 30) or operate barangay day-to-day affairs.
- Software language: use "review," "monitor," "ensure compliance," "provide assistance" — not "approve all barangay actions."

## Official identifiers

- **PSGC** — Philippine Standard Geographic Code (barangay/municipality/province codes)
- **LGU income classification** — DBM-determined; affects procurement thresholds
- **Term of office** — 3 years for elective local officials (sync across LGU levels after 2018)
